## Prompts finales

### Hogar
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: landscape website gallery photograph for Innovar Security, a Colombian CCTV installation business. Generate a cinematic photorealistic architectural scene of a welcoming contemporary rural Colombian house at blue hour, a discreet unbranded white outdoor security camera mounted beneath the eave clearly visible in foreground, warm interior lights, garden and mountain silhouettes. Premium editorial photography, realistic installation, navy and cyan dusk tones matching a dark technology website. Wide landscape 3:2 composition. No people, text, logos, watermarks, screens, UI or surveillance beams. Conceptual illustrative scene, not evidence of an actual customer installation.

### Negocio
Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: landscape gallery photograph for a Colombian CCTV installation website. Premium cinematic architectural photograph of a small contemporary neighborhood shop in Colombia after sunset, neat glass storefront with warm interior lighting, discreet unbranded white CCTV camera high on facade prominently visible, blue hour navy and cyan palette, no people. Realistic professional installation. 3:2 wide composition. No readable signs, logos, text, watermarks, UI, surveillance beams. Concept image, not an actual customer installation.

### Cámara
Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: landscape gallery photograph for a premium CCTV installation website. Cinematic macro product photograph of an elegant unbranded white and graphite outdoor turret security camera with detailed optical glass lens, mounted on a textured dark architectural wall, shallow depth of field, soft cyan rim light and navy background, realistic materials and proportions. 3:2 horizontal composition. No brands, text, logos, watermarks, UI or laser beams. Illustrative product concept.

## Funcionamiento
Carga diferida y tamaños adaptables con Next Image. Las imágenes se amplían a petición del usuario. El diálogo nativo mantiene el foco dentro, permite Escape para cerrar y devuelve el foco al disparador. Flechas, botones y gesto horizontal permiten navegar. El movimiento respeta prefers-reduced-motion.
